2. Grid animation options

Click the shuffle label on each card (or the replay button) to play. All effects are ~0.4–0.7s.

A · Whisper fade

Pure opacity, tight diagonal stagger. The quietest option — no motion, just presence. Best for a very restrained portfolio.

↺Shuffle
B · Focus in

Blur → sharp with a hint of scale. Like a photograph developing. No directional motion.

↺Shuffle
C · Soft rise

Slide up from below with a clean ease-out. No tilt, no spring bounce. Confident and calm.

↺Shuffle
D · Elastic pop

Scale from 0.85 with a spring overshoot. Playful — feels like tiles snapping into place.

↺Shuffle
E · Curtain wipe

Each tile reveals top-down via clip-path. Reads like a page loading. Editorial, quiet.

↺Shuffle
F · Ripple from center

Center tile first, then outward in rings. The stagger is spatial, not linear.

↺Shuffle
G · Soft drift

Each tile drifts in from a random small direction (±6px). Feels natural, never metronomic.
